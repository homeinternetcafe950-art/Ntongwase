Privacy notice

This notice explains how we handle the details you give us through the quote form, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

  • Who is responsible: Ntongwase Holdings, Brits, North West. Contact us on 073 455 4160, 066 035 0677 or stanleyphelembe4@gmail.com.
  • What we collect: your name, phone number, an email address if you add one, the service you choose and the job details you type.
  • Why: only to reply to your enquiry and give you a quote for the work you ask about.
  • How the form works: this website does not receive or store what you type. When you press the button, your browser builds a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. If you prefer not to use the form, call or email us instead.
  • WhatsApp: once you press Send, the message travels through WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy, and may be processed outside South Africa. We see your name and phone number in WhatsApp when it arrives.
  • Sharing: we use your details only to deal with your enquiry. We do not share them with anyone else unless the law requires it.
  • Giving us your details is your choice. Without your name and phone number we cannot reply by phone or WhatsApp. Please do not put ID numbers, banking details or other sensitive information in the message.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or remove it, object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Contact us using the details above.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za or POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
